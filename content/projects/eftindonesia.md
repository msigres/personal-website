# EFT Indonesia

![EFT Indonesia](/images/eftindonesia.png)


The EFT Indonesia Platform is a digital knowledge hub that supports collaboration, data-driven policymaking, and public participation in driving inclusive and sustainable fiscal transformation across Indonesia.

EFT Indonesia is a digital platform designed to collect, manage, and disseminate information about Ecology initiatives in Indonesia. Through this platform, users can access data, publications, adoption maps, and interactive media related to programs such as TAPE, TAKE, and ALAKE.

EFT Indonesia is not just an information center, but a collaborative space, a place where data, ideas, and real actions meet for a more inclusive Green Indonesia future.

Website: [eftindonesia.org](https://eftindonesia.org/)
